How the Narainsons app works, from start to finish

The full process from a customer's first login to the last EMI of their loan, and who does what at each stage.

The three parts of the system

Customer: mobile app

The customer signs in, completes KYC, applies for a loan and sends proof of EMI payments.

Staff: admin panel

Your team works in a browser to check KYC, approve loans, record money sent and verify EMI payments.

System: server

It stores all data, checks the rules, calculates EMIs, and sends OTPs, push notifications and reminders.

The whole journey at a glance

Login→Profile and KYC→KYC check→Loan application→Approval→Money sent→EMI payment→Loan closed

Money does not move through the app

The app has no payment gateway at this stage. Staff send the loan amount from your bank and enter the UTR number in the admin panel. Customers pay EMIs by UPI, QR code or bank transfer and upload a screenshot in the app. Staff then check the bank and approve the payment.

CustomerGets a notification and sees the EMI dates in the app.
StaffSends the money from the bank, then clicks Disburse in the admin panel and enters the UTR number.

7

EMI payment

CustomerGets the UPI, QR or bank details from the app and pays. Sends the screenshot and UTR in the app.
StaffChecks the payment in the bank and approves or rejects it. Can add a reason for rejection.
SystemRefuses a claim below the EMI amount. Allows only one pending proof per EMI. Sends the result by push and counts the remaining EMIs.
8

Reminders

CustomerSees the reminder and pays the EMI on time.
StaffNothing to do
SystemChecks every hour on its own. Sends one reminder a day, starting 3 days before the due date. After the due date it marks the EMI "overdue" and keeps reminding.
9

Loan closed

CustomerGets a notification. Can see the old loan's record and apply for a new loan.
StaffOnly needs to approve the last EMI
SystemCloses the loan automatically when the last EMI is approved.

Loan status

Pending→Approved→Disbursed→Closed
PendingApplication received, waiting for staff to decide
ApprovedStaff accepted it, money not sent yet
DisbursedMoney sent, EMIs are running
ClosedAll EMIs are paid
RejectedStaff declined it
CancelledThe customer cancelled it

EMI status

Pending→Awaiting proof→Paid
PendingStill to be paid
Awaiting proofCustomer sent proof, staff will check it
PaidStaff approved the payment
OverdueThe due date passed without payment

If staff reject the proof, the EMI returns to pending, or to overdue if the due date has passed.

What's in the admin panel

  • Dashboard: work waiting, the loan book and activity by date. Each card opens its list.
  • Notifications: staff alerts, messages sent to customers, and sending a new message
  • Customers: customer details and KYC review
  • Loan applications: approve, reject and disburse
  • Guarantors, EMI payments, EMI schedule: check proof and see what is overdue
  • Home banners, payment details, videos: what appears in the app
  • Staff accounts, All data, My profile: staff and records

Every list has search, date and advanced filters, 25 to 500 rows per page, and CSV or Excel download.

When a new customer signs up, a loan is applied for, KYC is submitted or EMI proof arrives, staff get a loud alert in the panel.

Data security

  • Passwords are never stored as plain text, only as a secure hash.
  • 5 wrong OTPs cancel the OTP. 5 wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes.
  • Repeated OTP requests are limited, which controls SMS cost and misuse.
  • Changing a password signs the user out of all other devices. An admin login lasts 12 hours.
  • Only the customer and your staff can see that customer's Aadhaar, PAN and payment screenshots.
  • The app does not store customers' contacts or gallery. This setting is turned off.
