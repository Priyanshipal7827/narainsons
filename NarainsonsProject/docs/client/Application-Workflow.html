Narainsons app shuru se aakhir tak kaise kaam karti hai

Customer ke pehle login se lekar loan ki aakhri EMI tak ka poora process, aur har stage par kaun kya karta hai.

System ke teen hisse

Customer: mobile app

Customer app se login karta hai, KYC deta hai, loan apply karta hai aur EMI ka proof bhejta hai.

Staff: admin panel

Aapki team browser se KYC check karti hai, loan approve karti hai, paise bhejne ki entry karti hai aur EMI verify karti hai.

System: server

Sara data save karta hai, rules check karta hai, EMI ka hisaab lagata hai, aur OTP, push notification aur reminder bhejta hai.

Poora safar ek nazar mein

Login→Profile aur KYC→KYC check→Loan apply→Approval→Paise bheje→EMI payment→Loan close

Paisa app ke andar se nahi jata

Abhi app mein payment gateway nahi hai. Staff loan ka paisa apne bank se bhejta hai aur uska UTR number admin panel mein darj karta hai. Customer EMI UPI, QR ya bank transfer se bharta hai aur screenshot app mein upload karta hai. Staff bank mein check karke use approve karta hai.

Admin panel mein kya hai

  • Dashboard: pending kaam, loan ka hisaab aur date-wise activity. Har card apni list kholta hai.
  • Notifications: staff alerts, customers ko bheje gaye message, aur naya message bhejna
  • Customers: customer ki details aur KYC review
  • Loan applications: approve, reject aur disburse
  • Guarantors, EMI payments, EMI schedule: proof check karna aur overdue dekhna
  • Home banners, payment details, videos: app mein dikhne wali cheezein
  • Staff accounts, All data, My profile: staff aur records

Har list mein search, date aur advanced filter hain, 25 se 500 rows per page, aur CSV ya Excel download.

Naya customer, naya loan, KYC submit ya EMI proof aate hi staff ko panel mein tez awaaz ke saath alert milta hai.

Data ki suraksha

  • Password seedhe save nahi hota, uska encrypted hash save hota hai.
  • 5 galat OTP par OTP cancel ho jata hai. 5 galat password par account 15 minute ke liye lock hota hai.
  • OTP baar baar bhejne ki limit hai, jisse SMS ka kharcha aur galat use rukta hai.
  • Password badalne par baaki sab devices se login hat jata hai. Admin ka login 12 ghante chalta hai.
  • Aadhaar, PAN aur payment screenshot sirf wahi customer aur aapka staff dekh sakta hai.
  • App customer ke contacts aur gallery save nahi karti. Yeh setting band rakhi gayi hai.
